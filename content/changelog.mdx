# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [2026-01-07]

### Added
- **Smarter Documentation Sidebar**: Sections now collapse automatically to help you stay focused on the content you're reading.
- **Typo-Tolerant Search**: You can now find documentation even if you make small spelling mistakes.
- **Visual Search Highlights**: Search terms are now highlighted in results and on the page to help you find answers faster.
- **Direct Navigation**: Search results now take you exactly to the relevant section of a page.
- **New Installation Guides**: Step-by-step instructions for adding Quran Life to your iPhone, Android, or Desktop for offline use.
- **Reliable Data Sync**: Improved background syncing and a new, clearer window to help you resolve conflicts between devices.
- **New Progress Chart**: Visualize your memorization depth with the "Similar Verse Coverage" chart in the Statistics tab.
- **Better Mobile Experience**: Improved layouts for phones and tablets, plus new live previews for your mindmaps.
- **Smoother Start**: Refined the onboarding process for new users with a better progress tracker.

### Changed
- **Simpler Documentation**: Completely rewritten guides using clearer language and a new "Philosophy vs. Application" structure.
- **Optimized Memorization Workflow**: Updated recommendations to use mindmaps earlier in your process, helping you save time on rote memorization.
- **Smart Syncing**: The app now syncs automatically when you open it, with a handy new toolbar button to show your status.
- **Smarter Daily Portions**: Improved how the app divides your daily reading, including proper "Isti3atha" display for mid-surah starts.
- **Clearer Review Actions**: Better "Not Sure" button guidance and automatic flagging of similar verses (Mutashabihat) when you make a mistake.
- **Refined Settings**: Easier surah skipping for mastered texts and improved controls in the mindmap builder.

### Fixed
- **Sync & Offline**: Fixed offline access issues and improved the accuracy of sync counts.
- **Mobile Layout**: Polished the mobile interface, fixing alignment issues and header sizes.
- **Stability**: Fixed a crash in the mindmap viewer.

### Removed
- **Clean Up**: Removed outdated settings and old, unnecessary sync descriptions.

## [2025-12-27]

### Fixed
- **Reliability**: Resolved several critical issues that caused the app to crash or show a blank screen during mindmap editing.
- **Performance**: Fixed various technical bugs to ensure the interface remains stable and responsive.

## [2025-12-26]

### Added
- **Refined Review Experience**: Enhanced the daily review interface for a smoother, more focused experience.
- **Improved Offline Support**: The app is now more reliable when you're using it without an internet connection.
- **New Mobile Layouts**: Added mobile-optimized views for progress charts and documentation.

### Changed
- **Navigation Polish**: Smoother interactions and layout improvements for the Settings and Todo pages.

## [2025-12-25]

### Added
- **Detailed Progress Tracking**: See exactly how well you know each verse with the new "Memory Nodes" section in settings.
- **Native Look & Feel**: Updated app icons for a better experience when Quran Life is installed on your home screen.
- **Mindmap Zoom**: You can now zoom in and out while editing your mindmaps.

### Changed
- **New Name**: We've officially renamed the application to **Quran Life**!
- **Clearer Terminology**: Changed "Maturity" to "Knowledge Tracking" to better reflect your memorization journey.
- **Optimized Mobile Layout**: Removed redundant borders and clutter to make better use of your phone's screen space.
- **Daily Reading Improvements**: Refined how daily portions are calculated for a more natural reading flow.

### Fixed
- **Cloud Saving**: Fixed a bug where progress wouldn't always save correctly to your account.

## [2025-12-24]

### Added
- **Initial Release**: Quran Life is now live! Featuring cloud sync, progress tracking, and PWA support for all your devices.

### Fixed
- **Sync Reliability**: Fixed a rare issue where the app would sync repeatedly in a loop.
- **Visuals**: Resolved issues with dark mode settings not being saved correctly.
