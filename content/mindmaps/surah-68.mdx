# 68. Al-Qalam

- Verses 1-7: The prophet is not crazy; his character has been proven by his exemplary behavior before Islam and by both the old scriptures and the future Sunnah books.
- Verses 8-16: On the opposite spectrum, those who oppose him possess terrible character.
- Verses 17-33: They are deluded like the people of the gardens who achieved worldly ruin through their ingratitude.
- Verses 34-47: Their current delusion will bring them otherworldly ruin if they continue on their present path.
- Verses 48-end: So be patient, and don't be like Prophet Yunus, who gave up before his allotted time.
