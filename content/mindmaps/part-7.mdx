# Part 7

Allah has sent prophets to warn and to guide us to Him (67). He sent the Prophet (68) and other past messengers (69). However, they were denied; their people rejected accountability and its punishment (70), and they denied the oneness of God by continuing to worship people (71) or jinn (72). Therefore, the current prophet (Muhammad) needs to prepare (73) for this rejection before beginning his call to Allah (74).

The call is essential for people to enact change and guide themselves back to Allah. Change occurs in two steps: stopping the bad and initiating the good.

- Stopping the bad involves stopping the bad beliefs and stopping the bad actions that people are involved in.
	- The bad beliefs that needed to be changed are :
		- Frivolity : (I can do whatever I want without consequences), even though they are accountable (75) and will have a great destiny after this accountability (76)
		- Doubt of this reality. They can deny it (77) or question it (78) all they want, it is happening
		- Pride because they are people of high status in society. Just as those of high status in the past were punished (79), you too are not protected by your status and should therefore not be prioritised (80).
	- The bad deeds that needed to be stopped are :
		- Having partners with Allah by following superstitions (81), they should cease it (82).
		- sinning repeatedly without care (83); they should remember their fate and that there is no escape for them (84).
		- harming others, similar to what people in the past did (85) or what you are planning right now against the prophet (86)
- Beginning the good involves having good intentions and good actions.
	- Good intentions mean seeking the right goal, the right path, and the right mindset.
		- The right goal is to seek and love Allah through his attributes (87,88) while leaving/detaching yourself from the world (89)
		- You have to choose the higher path of difficulty (90), not the path of doom (91), and whichever path you take will be made easy for you (92)
		- Having the right mindset means acknowledging his help through his blessings (93), that after hardship comes ease (94), and that there is wisdom in it (95).
	- Good actions are submission and preparation
		- submission to Allah by recognising that He is the ultimate reality behind everything (96) and following His words (97) without objection (98). More practically described in (103).
		- Preparation for the Day of Judgment, specifically two events: the accounting of deeds (99) through being careful about the quality of your deeds (100), and the scale (101) by being careful about the quantity of your deeds (102). This represents the opposite of the unprepared person described in (104), who neglected the quality and quantity of his deeds.

After the call to Allah, we see the result of this call through the example of the people of Quraysh: Allah gave protection from the people of the elephant (105) to Quraysh (106). However, their reaction to this blessing was not appropriate (107), even though they should have worshiped him out of gratitude (108). From this, we can clearly see the difference between the two groups (109): the believers will be victorious (110), and the disbelievers will be defeated (111).
