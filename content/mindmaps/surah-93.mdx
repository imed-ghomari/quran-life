# 93. Ad-Duhaa

- Verses 1-8: The Prophet is void of life when the revelation is paused; nevertheless, Allah did not abandon him. The proof of that is his countless past blessings (shelter, guidance, self-sufficiency).
- Verses 9-end: Therefore, he must show gratitude by caring for those in states he once occupied (orphans and seekers).
