# 72. Al-Jinn

- Verses 1-3: The jinn were trying to listen to the Prophet's recitation of the Quran.
- Verses 4-18: This is because they attempted to intercept this revelation from the sky, which they had done in the past, but not anymore.
- Verses 19-end: The only knowledge he can share with them is the revealed knowledge from his Lord.
