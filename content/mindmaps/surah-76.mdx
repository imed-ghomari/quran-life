# 76. Al-Insan

- Verses 1-3: Allah created the human being from nothing to become something worthy of a great destiny.
- Verses 4-22: Either the fire or paradise. In paradise, there are multiple levels: in the first level, you are responsible for getting the drink yourself; in the second level, you are given the drink; and in the third (highest) level, Allah gives you the drink.
- Verses 23-end: This great destiny requires patience and worship to attain.
