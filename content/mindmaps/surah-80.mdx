# 80. 'Abasa

- Verses 1-16: The Prophet was reprimanded for prioritizing the leaders of Quraysh (disbelievers) over a believer.
- Verses 17-32: They should not have been prioritized because they did not believe in the Hereafter. They don't even consider how the lifecycle of their own food resembels the lifecycle of their lives.
- Verses 33-end: Even though they do not care about the Day of Judgment now, on that day it will be the only matter they care about, and nothing else—even their families—will matter to them.
