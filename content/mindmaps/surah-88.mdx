# 88. Al-Ghashiyah

- Verses 1–16: On the Day of Resurrection, people will be divided into two destinies. It is called "the overwhelming event" because nothing is more important than this, including …
- Verses 17–20: This worldly life, which is meant for reflection and serving as evidence for the Day of Resurrection.
- Verses 21–end: The Prophet’s role is solely to remind people of this reality—nothing more.
