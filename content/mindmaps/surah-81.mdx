# 81. At-Takwir

- Verses 1-9: When the sun loses its light (day of resurrection)
- Verses 10-18: You who use the sky for your superstitions, you will understand the real truth
- Verses 19-end: And that is the truth delivered by the powerful angel and the most perfect human being (the prophet)
