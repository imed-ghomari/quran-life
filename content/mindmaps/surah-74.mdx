# 74. Al-Muddaththir

- Verses 1-7: The prophet is instructed to begin the propagation of the message.
- Verses 8-31: He is to warn the people of Quraysh about the punishment, emphasising that they cannot overpower the angels who will cast them into the fire.
- Verses 32-end: They are advised to cease their wrongdoings and to prepare for that day.
