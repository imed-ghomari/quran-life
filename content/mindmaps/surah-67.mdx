# 67. Al-Mulk

- Verses 1-5: The ones who own everything created life on this earth as a way to test us.
- Verses 6-14: This test will result in either failure or success in the afterlife.
- Verses 15-24: Life on this earth is therefore not the end goal we should pursue.
- Verses 25-end: Those who do so will fail the test.
