# 83. Al-Mutaffifin

- Verses 1-6: The criminals who sin repeatedly do so because they lack any morality that would come from the belief in the day of judgment.
- Verses 7-18: As a result, they will find themselves with their book of deeds in the hellfire's lowest prison.
- Verses 19-end: In contrast, the good people are in an elevated state, where the angels admire their book of deeds.
