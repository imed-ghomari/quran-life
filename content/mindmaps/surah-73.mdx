# 73. Al-Muzzammil

- Verses 1-11: The prophet and his companions are instructed to prepare for the next stage of their journey through night prayer.
- Verses 12-19: This will help them to maintain their patience against the disbelievers, who have a poor destination.
- Verse 20: However, not all companions could uphold the command; therefore, Allah granted them some flexibility.
