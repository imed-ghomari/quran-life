# 75. Al-Qiyamah

- Verses 1-15: The change between the two opposing emotions of following our instinct and then feeling guilty about it is proof of having accountability that will take place on the Day of Judgment.
- Verses 16-19: The prophet is different from them; he rushes, trying to seek Allah.
- Verses 20-25: While they rush towards their bad deeds.
- Verses 26-end: Until death overtakes them.
