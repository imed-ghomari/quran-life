# 77. Al-Mursalat

- Verses 1–15: Through a single element—the wind—Allah can bring about both life and death. Therefore, resurrecting us is easy for Him.
- Verses 16–19: Reflect on how previous nations were destroyed because of their denial.
- Verses 20–28: Observe how life is created and sustained.
- Verses 14–end: So do not deny the two destinies that follow the resurrection.
