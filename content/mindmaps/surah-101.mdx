# 101. Al-Qari'ah

- Verses 1-5: The Day of Judgment will come knocking loudly, changing the reality of things to reveal the true importance of matters.
- Verses 6-end: One of the most important things is our deeds, which will be measured by the scale that will determine our destiny.
