# 89. Al-Fajr

- Verses 1–5: There are five situations in which the afterlife should be prioritised over this worldly life by those who truly understand its importance.
- Verses 6–20: This priority can be achieved by changing one’s perspective on money/possessions: avoiding wasteful spending, not tying one’s identity to wealth, and not viewing wealth as the ultimate goal.
- Verses 21–end: Otherwise, you will regret not having worked for your true life in the hereafter.
