# 89. Al-Fajr

- Verses 1-5: There are five times when the afterlife should be especially prioritised over this life for those who understand its importance.
- Verses 6-14: This can be achieved by avoiding wasteful expenditure.
- Verses 15-16: Or by associating wealth with one's identity.
- Verses 17-end: Or by viewing wealth as the ultimate goal, because the ultimate aim is the two destinies in the afterlife.
