# 78. An-Naba

- Verses 1-5: They question the great news that will shake them because it entails that actions have consequences.
- Verses 6-16: However, it doesn't make sense not to have any accountability in such a perfectly balanced world.
- Verses 17-end: Therefore, a change is needed, and we must work to earn the right to enter the blessed destination and avoid the wretched one.
