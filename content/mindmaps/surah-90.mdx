# 90. Al-Balad

- Verses 1–4: Human life is challenging, as illustrated by the Prophet’s experiences in Makkah.
- Verses 5–end: This suffering can either be alleviated in this life through the good use of available resources, or faced in the next life as a consequence of failing to address these difficulties in this world.
