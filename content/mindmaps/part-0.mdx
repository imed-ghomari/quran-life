# Part 0 (Meta Mindmap)

This page documents the **Part 0 Meta Mindmap** used as a global orientation layer.

- It explains relationships across **Parts 1-7**.
- It appears as a dedicated card in the **Todo Kanban**.
- It is available regardless of the **Active Part** selected in Settings.

## Explanation

Part 0 is a structural map, not a replacement for individual part mindmaps.  
Use it to understand cross-part flow, major transitions, and high-level thematic links before or during detailed review.

## Notes

- The current Part 0 canvas starts blank by design so you can build it yourself.
- Review behavior is aligned with other part mindmaps in Todo.
