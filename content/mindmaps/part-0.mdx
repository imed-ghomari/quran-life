# Part 0 (Meta Mindmap)

Part 0 serves as a structural overview, not a replacement for the individual part mindmaps. Use it to grasp cross-part flow, major transitions, and high-level thematic links before or alongside detailed review.

Inspired by the Farahi/Islahi school of thought (Tadabbur-i-Qur'an), this framework arranges the Qur'an into seven groups, each built around a specific "Pillar" (central theme). These seven groups are aligned with the three levels of religion described in the **Hadith of Jibril** to make the structure easier to absorb and remember.

These seven categories constitute the guidance we ask for in Surah Al-Fātiḥah (1), and we ask Allah to remove the barriers to attaining it in Surahs 112–114.

## 1. Category: ISLAM (Parts 1–3)

**Focus:** The outward dimension of religion—law, community, and history.

- **Group 1 (Surahs 1–5): THE LAW.** _Building the Muslim community and defining its rules._
- **Group 2 (Surahs 6–9): THE STRUGGLE.** _The victory over polytheism and the purification of faith._
- **Group 3 (Surahs 10–24): THE PROOF.** _Stories of past nations that confirm the truth of the Prophet's message._

## 2. Category: IHSAN (Part 4)

**Group 4 (Surahs 25–33): THE SOUL.** _Refining our character, our homes, and our inner intentions (ethics)._ 

## 3. Category: IMAN (Parts 5–7)

**Focus:** The inward dimension of belief in Allah and the Next Life.

- **Group 5 (Surahs 34–49): THE SIGNS.** _Arguments from nature and history for the Oneness of God._
- **Group 6 (Surahs 50–66): THE HEREAFTER.** _Preparing for the meeting with Allah and the Day of Judgment._
- **Group 7 (Surahs 67–114): THE WARNING.** _The final, rhythmic warning to humanity about their ultimate destiny._
