# 87. Al-A'la

- Verse 1: Allah is the most high, the most perfect
- Verses 2-5: He created the two extreme destinations to display His power.
- Verses 6-8: And He gave us the Quran.
- Verses 9-end: And He guided us through it. Those who follow will attain success, and those who do not will fail.
