# How part mindmaps and surah mindmaps should work together

Part mindmaps and surah mindmaps do not compete. They operate at different scales.

A surah map gives local structure. A part map gives large-scale orientation, transitions, and continuity across a broader section of Quran.

This matters because students often lose stability not only inside one surah, but also at the boundaries between surahs and themes.

The part map helps you remember the bigger road while the surah map helps you navigate the smaller streets.
