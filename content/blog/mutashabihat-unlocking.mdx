# Why mutashabihat should unlock when the verse is relevant, not all at once

If similar verses are shown too early, they become noise. If they are shown too late, confusion has already hardened.

The right moment is when a verse becomes active enough in your workflow that comparison is useful rather than overwhelming. Quran Life tries to bring mutashabihat into view when it can actually help memory, not just add one more list to study.

This is an important design choice. More information is not always better.

A student does not need every possible confusion case on day one. They need the right distinction at the right moment.
