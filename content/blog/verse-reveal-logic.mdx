# Why Quran Life hides the verse before showing it

The reveal button is not a cosmetic interaction. It exists to force recall before confirmation.

If you see the verse too early, your brain switches from memory retrieval to recognition, and that feels easier than it really is. Quran Life tries to protect the hard part of memorization: the moment where you must pull the ayah out of your own memory.

That is why the reveal flow fits the review model instead of bypassing it. First you attempt. Then you reveal. Then you grade honestly.

The value is not in hiding text for drama. The value is in preserving the difference between "I knew it" and "I recognized it when I saw it."
