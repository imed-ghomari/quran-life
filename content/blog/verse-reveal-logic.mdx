# Why Quran Life hides the verse before showing it

The reveal button is not a cosmetic interaction. It exists to force recall before confirmation.

If you see the verse too early, your brain switches from memory retrieval to recognition, and that feels easier than it really is. Quran Life tries to protect the hard part of memorization: the moment where you must pull the ayah out of your own memory.

The app also does not reveal the verse as one giant block by default. It tries to reveal it in smaller contextual chunks. Those chunks use waqf-aware breaks where possible so the meaning stays intact, and then apply a 10-word maximum when a chunk is still too long. That balance matters because revealing too many words at once makes grading easier in the wrong way.

So the reveal logic is balancing two things at the same time: preserving enough meaning to make the wording readable, and keeping the chunk small enough that recall is still being tested honestly.
