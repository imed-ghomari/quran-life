# When should you expand a surah mindmap further?

A map does not need maximum detail from the beginning. Too much detail too early can make the map heavy and artificial.

The better approach is to start with enough structure to carry the flow, then add more detail where confusion, weak transitions, or repeated mistakes actually demand it.

That keeps the map alive and useful.

You are not decorating it for completeness. You are deepening it where memory proves it is needed.
