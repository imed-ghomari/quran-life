# Inside the mindmap editor: why Quran Life treats mapping as real memorization work

The mindmap editor is not there to decorate your hifdh. It is where a surah starts becoming structured in your memory.

Instead of keeping the page as one long undifferentiated block, you turn it into branches, transitions, and relationships that your brain can revisit later during review.

This is also why Quran Life puts real weight on editing and saving the map. The map is not a side note added after memorization. In this method, the map is part of memorization itself.

It gives the verses a place to live.
