# What mutashabihat resolution states are really tracking

A mutashabihat item is not simply solved or unsolved. There is a difference between noticing a similarity, understanding the logic behind it, and trusting yourself under pressure.

Resolution states matter because they separate awareness from mastery.

That makes the feature more honest. Instead of pretending one quick comparison finishes the problem, Quran Life lets you move through stages of certainty.

This mirrors real hifdh more closely: first you notice the issue, then you reason through it, then you prove it in review.
