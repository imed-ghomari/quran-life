# What mutashabihat resolution states are really tracking

A mutashabihat item in Quran Life is not tracked by one vague "done" flag. The app stores three things around it: the status you selected, any notes you wrote, and whether it has a confirmation timestamp.

The selectable statuses are mainly there to describe how you are treating the issue right now. For example, you might mark it as something solved through mindmap logic, something solved through notes, or something ignored because it is not truly a confusing similarity for you. But the important technical line is this: an item only counts as resolved in the app once it is either confirmed or explicitly ignored.

That means the app is not really modeling a philosophical ladder of hidden stages. It is modeling practical review state. Have you chosen an approach yet? Did you write anything useful? And has this similarity actually been confirmed strongly enough to stop counting as unresolved?

That is more concrete, and it lines up better with what the interface is actually doing.
