# What review order should you choose, and when?

Review order changes the feel of your session.

In general, `Due Date (Reviewed First)` is the normal default and the generally preferred mode. New users start there, and that makes sense because it usually gives the best balance between urgency and flow: reviewed cards come first, then the rest continue by due date.

The other modes are still useful. A more grouped mode can make review feel more locally coherent when you want to stay inside one surah's flow. A type-grouped mode can help when you want clearer separation between part maps, surah maps, and verse work.

So the best way to think about it is simple: stay on `Due Date (Reviewed First)` unless you are feeling a specific friction that grouped-by-surah or type-grouped order would solve better.
