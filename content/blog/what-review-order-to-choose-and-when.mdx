# What review order should you choose, and when?

Review order changes the feel of your session.

In general, the most practical mode is the one that behaves like old cards first and then newer work by due date. That is usually the most balanced option because it handles urgency without turning the session into something structurally confusing.

The other modes are still useful. A more grouped mode can make review feel more locally coherent when you want to stay inside one surah's flow. A type-grouped mode can help when you want clearer separation between part maps, surah maps, and verse work.

So the best way to think about it is simple: use the due-date style as your normal default, and switch to the other modes only when they solve a specific friction you are currently feeling.
