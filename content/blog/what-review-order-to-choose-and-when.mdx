# What review order should you choose, and when?

Review order changes the feel of your session. The best choice depends on whether you need coherence, urgency, or structural grouping.

If you want review to feel more natural and locally connected, grouped ordering often helps because related items stay near each other. If you want pure urgency, due-date order makes more sense. If you want to preserve the distinction between parts of the system, type grouping can be useful.

There is no universally perfect review order.

The right question is: what kind of friction am I trying to reduce in this season of memorization?
