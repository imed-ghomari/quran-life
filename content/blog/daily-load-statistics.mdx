# What daily load statistics are trying to show you

Daily load is not just about how much is due today. It is about how heavy your system feels before it becomes unmanageable.

Quran Life uses statistics to show whether your current review and study pattern is building a sustainable rhythm or quietly stacking too much pressure into future days.

This matters because hifdh often breaks down gradually, not dramatically.

A useful statistics panel helps you notice overload before the habit collapses. It turns "I feel behind" into something visible enough to adjust.
