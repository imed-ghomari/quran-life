# The plan for Quran Life: goals, user milestones, and what comes next

Quran Life is not being built as a generic productivity app with Islamic branding. It is trying to become a serious memorization system: one that joins visual structure, review timing, mutashabihat handling, and practical diagnosis in one workflow.

That means the roadmap should be judged by whether it makes long-term hifdh more stable, not by how many superficial features get added.

It is also healthy to be concrete about growth. How many committed users would prove this method is genuinely helping people? What features matter before scale, and what features only matter after trust is established?

A roadmap becomes stronger when the numbers and principles are stated together.
