# Create your own mindmap or use pre-built mindmaps?

Pre-built mindmaps give speed and structure. Building your own often gives deeper ownership.

That is why Quran Life supports both. A pre-built map can save energy and help you start from a tested structure. A custom map can strengthen memory because you are the one making the structural decisions.

The better choice depends on what you need right now. If you are blocked by having no structure, a pre-built map may be the best entry point. If you already understand the surah and want stronger personal grip, building or editing your own may go deeper.

The important thing is not which option looks more advanced. The important thing is whether the map becomes real in your memory.
