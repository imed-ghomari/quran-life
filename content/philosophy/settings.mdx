# Managing Your Journey (Settings)

The **Settings** tab is the control center for your memorization plan. Here, you tell the app what you want to achieve and what you already know.

## Define Your Scope

- **Active Part (Juz)**: This is your current focus area. The app filters your **Todo** list and **Daily Portion** to only show Surahs within this Part. You can change this at any time as you progress.
- **Target Completion**: How many days do you want to take to finish a full cycle of listening/reading your Active Part? This setting determines the size of your **Daily Portion**.

## Skipped Surah

This section allows you to manage Surahs you want to exclude from your daily cycle.

- **Skipped Surahs**: Search and add Surahs you want to skip (e.g., ones you know perfectly like Al-Fatiha or don't want to review yet). You can unskip them at any time to add them back to your review queue.

## Knowledge Tracking

This section allows you to quickly update the maturity of Surahs or mindmaps, individually or in bulk.

## Similar Coverage

- **Mutashabihat**: Helps you identify Surahs that contain many "Similar Verses" (Mutashabihat). You can see which ones are causing confusion and prioritize them.

## Data Management
You can also **Export** your data (mindmaps, review history, and settings) to a JSON file for backup or **Import** it on another device.
