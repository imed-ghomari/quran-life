# Managing Your Journey (Settings)

The **Settings** tab is the control center for your memorization plan. Here, you tell the app what you want to achieve and what you already know.

## Define Your Scope

- **Active Part (Juz)**: This is your current focus area. The app uses it to shape your **Daily Portion** and overall progression, while the **Todo** tab stays global so every card remains visible. Quran Life currently uses a [7-part structure](/docs/mindmaps/part-0), and you can change your active part at any time as you progress.
- **Target Completion**: How many days do you want to take to finish a full cycle of listening/reading your Active Part? This setting determines the size of your **Daily Portion**. You can also reset your daily portion progress here if needed.

## Skipped Surah

This section allows you to manage Surahs you want to exclude from your daily cycle and review section. You can unskip them at any time to add them back to your review queue.

## Knowledge Tracking

This section allows you to quickly check or update the maturity of Surahs or mindmaps, individually or in bulk. You can also filter by their state for quick navigation.

## Similar Coverage

This section helps you identify Surahs that contain many "Similar Verses" (Mutashabihat). You can see which ones are causing confusion and prioritize them.

## Advanced options

In this section, you can change some default behaviors of the app, such as the sorting of the kanban board or review queue, the default filter in the To-Do tab, or the default daily portion mode. You can also change what certain actions do, such as the effect of moving cards out of the "Complete" column on their related verse groups and mind maps.
