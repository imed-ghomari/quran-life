# Settings & Configuration

Tailor the application to your current memorization goals and manage your account.

## User Account (Cloud Sync)

Sign in to keep your memorization progress and mindmaps in sync across all your devices. 
- **Auto-Sync**: Your progress is automatically merged and uploaded when you are online.
- **Portability**: Access your mindmaps on desktop for creation and mobile for review.

## Active Learning Path

- **Active Part**: Focus your learning by selecting one of the 4 main Quranic portions. This filters all progress data and your Todo list.
- **Completion Days**: Set how many days you want to spend completing a full cycle of your active part. This determines the length of your **Daily Portion**.

## Knowledge Tracking

- **Learned Verses**: Toggle which surahs or specific ranges you have already memorized.
- **Skipped Surahs**: Mark surahs you wish to exclude entirely from cycles and statistics.
- **Bulk Mastery**: You can reset or set maturity for entire groups (e.g., all verses in a surah) using the 'Set Subgroup' buttons.
