# Managing Your Journey (Settings)

The **Settings** tab is the control center for your memorization plan. Here, you tell the app what you want to achieve and what you already know.

## Define Your Scope

- **Active Part (Juz)**: This is your current focus area. The app uses it to shape your **Daily Portion** and overall progression, while the **Practice** tab uses that scope for preparation and daily work. Quran Life currently uses a [7-part structure](/docs/mindmaps/part-0), and you can change your active part at any time as you progress.
- **Daily Portion Time**: How many minutes do you want to spend on your daily portion each day? Quran Life keeps that time target steady, then derives the cycle length from the currently eligible surahs in your Active Part. You can also reset your daily portion progress here if needed.

## Skipped Surah

This section allows you to manage Surahs you want to exclude from your daily cycle and review section. You can unskip them at any time to add them back to your review queue.

## Knowledge Tracking

This section allows you to quickly check or update the maturity of Surahs or mindmaps, individually or in bulk. You can also filter by their state for quick navigation.

## Similar Coverage

This section helps you identify Surahs that contain many "Similar Verses" (Mutashabihat). You can see which ones are causing confusion and prioritize them.

## Advanced options

In this section, you can change some default behaviors of the app, such as review queue sorting, the default daily portion mode, and how completed mindmaps and verse groups participate in review.
