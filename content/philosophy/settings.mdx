# Settings & Daily Portion

This is where you choose what you focus on each day.

## Daily Portion

You set these in the **Daily Portion** tab:

- **Active Part:** Choose the part you are working on. This decides which Surahs appear in your daily reading.
- **Daily Time:** Choose how many minutes you want to read or listen each day. The app keeps that time steady and tells you how many days you need to finish the part. You can also reset your progress here.

## Skipped Surahs

You can exclude Surahs you already know well from your daily reading. Add them back at any time. To skip a Surah for reviews, suspend it in Anki.

## Mindmaps and Splits

Mindmaps and splits are not in Settings. Open the **Anki Deck** tab, select a Surah, edit its mindmap or splits, then export.
