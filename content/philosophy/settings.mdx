# Customizing Your Experience (Settings)

The **Settings** tab is where you tailor the app to your specific goals and manage your account.

## Your Learning Path

- **Active Part**: Focus your learning by selecting a specific section of the Quran (e.g., Part 30). This filters your Todo list and statistics so you aren't overwhelmed.
- **Completion Days**: Choose how many days you want to take to complete a full cycle of your active part. This determines your **Daily Portion** size.

## Tracking What You Know

- **Learned Verses**: Toggle which Surahs you have already memorized. This adds them to your [Smart Review](/docs/philosophy/spaced-repetition) pool.
- **Skipped Surahs**: If you aren't ready for a specific Surah, you can "Skip" it. This removes it from your daily cycles and statistics.
- **Bulk Mastery**: Use the "Set Subgroup" buttons to quickly mark an entire Surah or Part as learned or unlearned.

## Your Account & Sync

Sign in to keep your progress safe.
- **Auto-Sync**: Your data is automatically backed up to the cloud.
- **Multi-Device**: Start a mindmap on your computer and finish it on your phone.
