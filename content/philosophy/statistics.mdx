# Tracking Your Progress

Your progress is shown in two places: your daily reading and your reviews.

## Understanding Memory Strength

As you review, your memory for each group gets stronger:

- **New**: Recently started or recently forgotten.
- **Medium**: Starting to stick in your long-term memory.
- **Strong**: Very solid retention.
- **Mastered**: Deeply known; you only need to see it every few months.

## What You Can Track

- **Daily Portion**: Shows how far you are in your current reading cycle.
- **Reviews**: See how well you know each group and how often you will see it again.

You can view your daily progress in the app and your review progress in Anki.

## Filters

Your daily progress respects your chosen Part and the Surahs you have chosen to skip.
