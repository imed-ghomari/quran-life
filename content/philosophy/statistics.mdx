# Tracking Your Progress

> **Branch update:** Verse/mindmap maturity and review forecast are now in **Anki Stats**, not the app's Statistics tab. Daily Portion progress stays in-app.

The **Statistics** tab previously gave you a bird's-eye view of your memorization journey. On this branch it is **not used** for verse reviews.

## Understanding Memory Strength (Maturity) - Now in Anki

Previously we used **Maturity Levels** to show the strength of your memory for each verse chunk. The more times you successfully remembered a verse, the "more mature" it became:
- **New**: Recently started or recently forgotten (reviewed every 1-14 days).
- **Medium**: Starting to stick in your long-term memory (reviewed every 14-30 days).
- **Strong**: Very solid retention (reviewed every 30-90 days).
- **Mastered**: Deeply encoded; you likely only need to see these once every few months.

The legend under each chart showed you exactly how many items fell into each of these buckets.

**On this branch:** See **Anki → Stats** for retention, interval, maturity. Anki's FSRS Desired Retention and deck options drive this.

## What You Can Track (Old App)

- **Verse Progress**: See the maturity of the verses. You could toggle between viewing by "Chunks" (detailed) or "Surahs" (aggregated).
- **Surah Mindmaps**: Track which Surahs had completed mindmaps and how mature those visual memories were.
- **Part Mindmaps**: A high-level view of your "Global" maps for entire Parts (Juz).
- **Daily Portion**: Shows your progress through your listening/reading cycles (how many Surahs completed vs. remaining in the current cycle).
- **Similar Verses (Mutashabihat)**: Tracked how many "Similar Verse" confusion points you had resolved (via Notes or Mindmaps) versus those still pending.
- **Review Plan**: Forecasted your review load over time and included a **Daily Load** reference line.
- **Daily Reviews**: A 180-day heatmap showing review consistency by day.
- **Weak Surahs**: Highlighted Surahs with the highest **mistake counts** in the selected range.

**On this branch:** Keep **Daily Portion** progress in the app (still shows surahs completed vs remaining). For verse/review stats, use **Anki Stats** and Browser searches like `tag:leech` `tag:mutashabihat` `tag:error::similar`.

## Accuracy & Filters
Previously the statistics were filtered by your **Active Part** and automatically excluded any Surahs you've marked as **Skipped** in the Settings. On this branch, Daily Portion stats still respect **Active Part** / **Skipped**; Anki stats respect whatever you suspended/tagged in Anki.
