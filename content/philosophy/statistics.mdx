# Tracking Your Progress (Statistics)

The **Statistics** tab gives you a bird's-eye view of your memorization journey. It's designed to show you not just *how much* you've learned, but *how well* you know it.

## Understanding Memory Strength (Maturity)

We use **Maturity Levels** to show the strength of your memory for each verse chunk. The more times you successfully remember a verse, the "more mature" it becomes:
- **New**: Recently started or recently forgotten (reviewed every 1-14 days).
- **Medium**: Starting to stick in your long-term memory (reviewed every 14-30 days).
- **Strong**: Very solid retention (reviewed every 30-90 days).
- **Mastered**: Deeply encoded; you likely only need to see these once every few months.

The legend under each chart shows you exactly how many items fall into each of these buckets.

## What You Can Track

- **Verse Chunks**: See the maturity of individual verses. You can toggle between viewing by "Chunks" (detailed) or "Surahs" (aggregated).
- **Surah Mindmaps**: Track which Surahs have completed mindmaps and how mature those visual memories are.
- **Part Mindmaps**: A high-level view of your "Global" maps for entire Parts (Juz).
- **Daily Portion**: Shows your progress through your listening/reading cycles (how many Surahs completed vs. remaining in the current cycle).
- **Similar Verses (Mutashabihat)**: Tracks how many "Similar Verse" confusion points you have resolved (via Notes or Mindmaps) versus those still pending.
- **Reviews**: This chart forecasts your review load for the coming days, weeks, or months, helping you plan your time. It also shows your current average number of reviews based on your past history.

## Accuracy & Filters
To give you the most accurate picture, the statistics are filtered by your **Active Part** and automatically exclude any Surahs you've marked as **Skipped** in the Settings.
