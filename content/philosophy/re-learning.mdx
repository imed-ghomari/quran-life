# Fixing Memory Gaps (Re-learning)

> **Branch update (Anki):** The automatic **3-Strike suspend** is replaced by **Leech / Flag manual**. There is no automatic **Re-learning Phase** inside the app.

Even with a perfect system, some verses will be harder to remember than others. When a specific verse chunk fails multiple times, it needs re-learning.

## The 3-Strike Rule (Now Manual)

Old app: If you made 3 errors in a row, anywhere inside the same verse group, the app would:

1. **Suspend the verse group**: It was removed from your daily review queue.
2. **Save your error**: It showed you the exact part of the verse group where the error happened whenever chunk-level context was available.

**On this branch:**

1. **Anki Leech**: Set Deck Options → Leech threshold **3**, Action **Tag Only**. After 3 **Again**, Anki adds tag `leech`.
2. **Manual**: You can also add **Flag Red** or tag `error::similar` yourself when you feel stuck. No automatic suspension; you decide.
3. **No chunk-level saved error**: The card's front is the whole group; after failing, review the group text and use **RelatedGroups** to see confusion source.

## How to Fix a Memory Gap

When an item is flagged/leeched, follow these steps in the **Anki Deck** tab:
- **Review Your Map**: Open **Edit Mindmap** for that Surah. Is the visual flow clear?
- **Update the Map**: Add a more detailed image or a better "story" for that verse chunk.
- **Refine the Split**: Use **Splits** → **Add anchor** / **Edit Splits** to split the difficult chunk into even smaller pieces, then **Save** and **Re-export** the surah.

## Why This Matters

This phase ensures you aren’t just “guessing” your way through reviews by building a solid, error-free foundation. Previously the app forced you to stop; now you make that call yourself via flag/leech and fix the underlying map/split before the next export.
