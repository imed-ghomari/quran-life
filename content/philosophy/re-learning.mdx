# Fixing Memory Gaps

Even with a perfect system, some verses will be harder to remember than others. When a group keeps causing mistakes, it needs extra attention.

## How to Know a Group Needs Fixing

If you keep marking the same group as not remembered, Anki will mark it for you so you know it needs fixing. You can also flag a card yourself when you feel stuck.

## How to Fix a Memory Gap

When a group needs fixing:

- **Review Your Map**: Open the mindmap for that Surah. Is the visual flow clear?
- **Update the Map**: Add a more detailed image or a better story for that part.
- **Refine the Split**: If a group feels too long, split it into smaller pieces, save, and export again.

## Why This Matters

This step keeps you from just guessing through reviews. Fixing the map or split builds a solid foundation so the memory sticks.
