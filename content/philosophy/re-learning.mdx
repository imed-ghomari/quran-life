# Fixing Memory Gaps (Re-learning)

Even with a perfect system, some verses will be harder to remember than others. When a specific verse chunk fails multiple times, the app moves it into a **Re-learning Phase**.

## The 3-Strike Rule

If you make 3 errors anywhere inside the same verse group in a row, the app will :

1. **Suspend the verse group**: It is removed from your daily review queue.
2. **Save your error**: It shows you the exact verse groups where you made the errors, so you know what to focus on.

## How to Fix a Memory Gap

When an item is suspended, follow these steps:
- **Review Your Map**: Open your mindmap for that Surah. Is the visual flow clear?
- **Update the Map**: Often, you just need a more detailed image or a better "story" for that verse chunk.
- **Refine the Split**: Use the Splits configuration modal to split the difficult chunk into even smaller pieces, if needed.

## Why This Matters

This phase ensures you aren’t just “guessing” your way through reviews by building a solid, error-free foundation for your memorization. It acts as a safety feature that prevents you from wasting time on a memory that isn’t sticking—rather than repeating the same mistake, you are forced to stop and fix the underlying issue.
