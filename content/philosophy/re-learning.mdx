# Fixing Memory Gaps (Re-learning)

Even with a perfect system, some verses will be harder to remember than others. When a specific verse chunk fails multiple times, the app moves it into a **Re-learning Phase**.

## The 3-Strike Rule

If you mark a **single verse group** as **Not Remembered** 3 times in a row, the app will:
1. **Suspend the Item**: It is removed from your daily "Today" reviews.
2. **Save your error**: It shows you the exact verse where you made the error, so you know what to focus on.

Important clarification: these 3 errors do **not** need to be in one exact verse. They can happen across different verses inside that same verse group. Suspension triggers only when all 3 errors are within one verse group.

This is a safety feature. It prevents you from wasting time on a memory that isn't sticking. Instead of repeating the same mistake, you are forced to stop and fix the underlying issue.

## How to Fix a Memory Gap

When an item is suspended, follow these steps:
- **Review Your Map**: Open your mindmap for that Surah. Is the visual flow clear?
- **Update the Map**: Often, you just need a more detailed image or a better "story" for that verse chunk.
- **Refine the Split**: Use the Splits configuration modal to split the difficult chunk into even smaller pieces, if needed.

## Why This Matters
This phase ensures you aren't just "guessing" your way through reviews. It builds a solid, error-free foundation for your memorization.
