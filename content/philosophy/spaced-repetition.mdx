# How Reviews Work

Reviews are managed by Anki using spaced repetition. You see cards just before you are about to forget them, so you remember longer with less effort.

## How a Review Feels

- **Step by Step:** Long verses are shown in small parts. Reveal the next part only after trying to recall it.
- **Recall First:** Try to say the verse from memory before you reveal it.
- **Grade Yourself:** After you have revealed the full group, choose Remembered, Not Remembered, or Postpone.

## Scheduling

Anki decides when you see a card next. As you keep remembering a group, you see it less often — from days to weeks to months. If you mark it as not remembered, you will see it sooner.

If you change how a Surah is split into groups, that group has changed. Export that Surah again and re-import; Anki will treat it as an updated card.

## What the Export Already Sets Up

The file you export already contains the card layout, the step-by-step reveal, and the scheduling settings (including marking a card when it is forgotten 3 times). After you import into Anki, open **Deck Options** and check that **FSRS** is enabled and **Desired Retention** is **0.90**. No other setup is needed — the rest works as imported.

## Tips

- Be honest when grading. It keeps the schedule accurate.
- Use **Space** to reveal the next part quickly on desktop.
