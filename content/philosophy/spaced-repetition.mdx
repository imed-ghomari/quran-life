# Smart Review System (Spaced Repetition)

Quran Life uses a **Smart Review System** (technically known as the SM-2 Algorithm) to manage your review schedule. This is the same science used by world-class language learning apps to ensure long-term memory.

## How Your Brain Remembers

Memories fade over time. However, if you review information just as you are about to forget it, that memory becomes significantly stronger. Each time you successfully remember a verse, the "gap" until your next review can be longer.

*Our system uses [Visual Mindmaps](/docs/philosophy/strategy) as the primary review unit to give your brain a physical 'place' to store these memories.*

## The Review Process

The smart system schedules reviews at optimal intervals to minimize your workload while maximizing retention.
- **Step-by-Step Revelation**: Long verses are shown word-by-word. This helps you check your recitation in small pieces, reducing the risk of missing minor errors.
- **Recall, Don't Read**: The goal is to recall the verse from memory *before* clicking to reveal it.
- **Grading Your Performance**: After revealing the verses, you must tell the app how you did:
    - **Forgot**: If you couldn't recall it or made a mistake.
    - **Remembered**: If you recalled it perfectly.
    - **Not Sure**: Use this if you're uncertain (e.g., you made a typo or were distracted). This postpones the review without resetting your progress entirely.
- **Reducing Workload**: Reviews are not daily for everything. As you get better, you'll see items less often—sometimes only once every few months.
- **Mastery**: Items eventually reach a "Mastered" state where you only need to review them once every few months.

## Why Reviews Sometimes Reset

Occasionally, a review interval might be reset to "Today." This happens to ensure your memory stays perfectly aligned with the app:
1. **Manual Changes**: If you mark a Surah as "To Learn" and then back to "Learned."
2. **Changing Verse Ranges**: If you change the start or end of a verse chunk. Because the "chunk" you memorized has changed, the app asks you to verify it again to be safe.
3. **Repeated Mistakes**: If you mark "Forgot" multiple times, the system will naturally show that item more frequently until you've mastered it again.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

For a faster and more focused review experience on desktop, you can use the following shortcuts:
- **Arrow Right**: Reveal the next verse chunk, or mark as **Remembered** when in the grading screen.
- **Arrow Left**: Mark as **Not Sure** (Postpone) when in the grading screen.
- **Arrow Down**: Mark as **Forgot** when in the grading screen.

## Smart Adjustments

We've customized the standard logic to better fit the Quran:
- **Flexible Postponing**: The "Not Sure" button lets you skip a review without the harsh penalty of a full reset.
- **Similar Verse Awareness**: The system can adjust your schedule if it detects you are confusing two similar-sounding passages. (*Learn more about [Similar Verses](/docs/philosophy/mutashabihat)*)

## Philosophy Tie-back
Spaced repetition ensures **minimal wasted effort**. You don't spend time reviewing what you already know well, and you don't lose progress on what you've just learned. The app handles the "when", so you can focus on the "what".
