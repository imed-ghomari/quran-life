# PWA and Syncing

To get the best experience on phone or tablet, we recommend installing Quran Life as a **PWA (Progressive Web App)**.

## Why PWA?

- **Offline Mode**: The PWA allows you to access your mindmaps and perform reviews even when you don't have an internet connection.
- **App-like Experience**: It removes the browser address bar and provides a more immersive, full-screen experience.
- **Fast Loading**: Core assets are cached locally, making the app feel snappier.

## Syncing and Cloud Backup

Quran Life uses a robust syncing system to keep your progress safe and consistent across all your devices.

### How it Works
1. **Local First**: All your changes are saved instantly to your device's local storage.
2. **Cloud Sync**: When an internet connection is available, the app periodically syncs your local data with your cloud account.
3. **Manual Sync**: You can always trigger a manual sync from the **Settings** tab.

### Sync Conflict Resolution
In cases where changes are made on two different devices while offline, a conflict might occur. Our workflow for resolution is as follows:
- **Timestamp Priority**: Generally, the most recent change takes precedence.
- **Intelligent Merging**: For progress data (like review counts or listening history), the app attempts to merge the data to ensure no progress is lost.
- **Manual Intervention**: If a significant conflict occurs (e.g., different versions of the same mindmap), the app will prompt you to choose which version to keep.

## Best Practices
- **Sync Before Switching**: If you plan to switch from your phone to your computer, ensure your phone has completed its sync.
- **Stay Updated**: Keep the app updated to ensure you have the latest improvements to the syncing engine.
