# Using Quran Life on Mobile (PWA & Sync)

For the best experience on a phone or tablet, we recommend installing Quran Life as a **PWA** (Progressive Web App).

## Why Install the App?

- **Works Offline**: Access your mindmaps and do your reviews even without an internet connection.
- **Full Screen**: Removes the browser bars for a more immersive experience.
- **Fast**: The app loads instantly because it stores core files on your device.

## Keeping Your Devices in Sync

Quran Life automatically keeps your progress safe and consistent across all your devices (phone, tablet, and computer).

### How Syncing Works
1. **Local First**: Every change you make is saved instantly to your device.
2. **Cloud Backup**: When you're online, the app silently sends those changes to your cloud account.
3. **Automatic Merging**: If you use two different devices, the app intelligently merges your progress so you never lose a review.

### What Happens if There's a Conflict?
If you make different changes on two devices while offline (like editing the same mindmap in two different ways), the app will:
- **Prioritize the Newest**: Generally, the most recent change is kept.
- **Ask You**: For major changes, the app will ask you which version you want to keep.

## Best Practice
If you're about to switch from your phone to your computer, just make sure your phone has had a moment to sync while online. You can check the sync status or trigger a manual sync in the **Settings** tab.
