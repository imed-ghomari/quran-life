# PWA and Syncing

For the best experience on any device—mobile, tablet, or desktop—we recommend installing Quran Life as a **PWA** (Progressive Web App).

## How to Install the App

### On iOS (iPhone/iPad)
1. Open the app in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an upward arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.

### On Android
1. Open the app in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three dots** in the top right corner.
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

### On Desktop (Chrome/Edge)
1. Open the app in your browser.
2. Look for the **Install** icon in the address bar (usually a small computer icon with an arrow).
3. Click **Install** to add Quran Life to your applications/desktop.

## Keeping Your Devices in Sync

Quran Life automatically keeps your progress safe and consistent across all your devices.

### How Syncing Works
- **Auto-Sync**: The app automatically attempts to sync your data whenever it **loads or reloads**.
- **Manual Sync**: You can trigger a manual sync at any time by clicking the **Sync icon** (two circular arrows) in the top toolbar. This is the best way to ensure your progress is saved before switching devices.
- **Conflict Resolution**: If the app detects different changes on two devices (e.g., you edited a mindmap on your phone and your tablet while offline), it will **ask you to manually choose** which version you want to keep to ensure no data is lost.
