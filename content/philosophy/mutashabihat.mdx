# Managing Similar Verses (Mutashabihat)

**Similar Verses** (known technically as *Mutashabihat*) are verses that look or sound almost identical. They are one of the most common challenges in Quran memorization. Quran Life helps you tackle them head-on.

## Awareness During Review

The app automatically identifies verses that have similar counterparts elsewhere. 
- **Smart Context**: When reviewing a similar verse, the app can provide extra context—automatically showing more of the verse or giving hints—until the point where the similarity ends.
- **Decision Making**: In the **Todo** tab, you'll see "Similarity Checks." This is where you decide how to handle each case (e.g., "I'll solve this with a Mindmap" or "I've added a personal note to distinguish them").

## How to Approach Similarities

You can handle similar verses in two main ways:

1. **Reactive (Errors during Review)**: This is the most efficient way. The app automatically flags similar verses in your **Todo** tab only when you make a mistake on a verse with a known similarity during a review. This ensures you only solve problems when they actually arise or become relevant.

2. **Proactive Study**: If you want to be thorough, you can find a complete list of all similarities for your active part in the [Settings](/docs/philosophy/settings) tab.

## The Best Way to Distinguish

When two verses are similar, it's tempting to use "tricks" (like remembering which letter comes first). However, we highly recommend using **logic**:
- **Thematic Flow**: Understand *why* a specific word is used based on the theme of the Surah. Logic is much harder to forget than a simple trick.
- **Visual Anchoring**: Place the similar verses in different locations on your [Mindmap](/docs/philosophy/mindmap-strategy) to create a visual distinction in your mind.

## Why This Matters
By addressing similar verses explicitly, you remove the "second-guessing" that often happens during recitation. Instead of being unsure, you make a conscious decision on how to tell them apart.
