# Managing Similar Verses (Mutashabihat)

> **Branch update (Anki):** Similar detection is now **tag-based manual**, not automatic remediation queue. Cards carry tag `mutashabihat` if the group has any related groups.

**Similar Verses** (known technically as *Mutashabihat*) are verses that look or sound almost identical. They are one of the most common challenges in Quran memorization. Quran Life helps you tackle them head-on.

## Awareness During Review

On this branch, cards with any mutashabihat carry tag `mutashabihat` and field `RelatedGroups` (e.g. `2:23 ~ 11:45`) listing up to 8 related groups. The card back shows this list, not word-range-filtered. The template bakes 5 context verses before start and shows 2 normally, expanding to 5 when `mutashabihat` is present. No automatic hints until similarity ends.

## How to Approach Similarities

You can handle similar verses in two main ways:

1. **Reactive (Errors during Review - Manual)**: When you fail a card and recognize confusion with a similar verse, add **Flag Red** or tag `error::similar` yourself. This replaces the app automatically flagging similar verses in the **Practice** remediation queue. You decide if it was similar-related.

2. **Proactive Study**: If you want to be thorough, search in Anki Browser `tag:mutashabihat` to see all groups with similarities. The builder bakes `RelatedGroups` at export time. No in-app [Settings](/docs/philosophy/settings) similarity list on this branch.

## What the Similarity Count Means (Old App)

When you saw a similarity card in **Practice**, the number was meant to answer: **How many comparison verses still need my attention right now?**

That is why the app did **not** count every raw match. Instead, it focused on actionable ones:

- **Ready now**: If the matching verse belonged to a Surah you already learned well enough.
- **Not ready yet**: If that matching verse belonged to a Surah you have not reached yet.
- **Still open**: If you have not solved that distinction yet.
- **Already handled**: If you marked it resolved or chose to ignore it.

**On this branch:** There is no dynamic count. Every card with any related group simply gets tag `mutashabihat`. To see how many are still flagged as errors, search Anki Browser `tag:mutashabihat tag:error::similar` or `flag:1`.

## The Best Way to Distinguish

When two verses are similar, it's tempting to use "tricks" (like remembering which letter comes first). However, we highly recommend using **logic**:
- **Thematic Flow**: Understand *why* a specific word is used based on the theme of the Surah. Logic is much harder to forget than a simple trick.
- **Visual Separation**: Place similar verses in different locations on your [Mindmap](/docs/philosophy/mindmap-strategy) to create a clear distinction in your mind.

## Why This Matters
By addressing similar verses explicitly, you remove the "second-guessing" that often happens during recitation. Instead of being unsure, you make a conscious decision on how to tell them apart.
