# Similar Verses

Similar Verses are verses that look or sound almost identical. They are one of the most common challenges in Quran memorization.

## How the App Helps

During review, cards that have known similarities will show you which groups are related, so you can keep them apart. Some cards may show a little more context before the verse to help you remember which one it is.

## How to Approach Similarities

1. **When you make a mistake**: If you failed because you confused two similar verses, mark the card so you can find it later and use the flag in Anki to keep track.
2. **To study ahead**: You can search for all cards that have similarities to see what to pay attention to next.

## The Best Way to Distinguish

When two verses are similar, it's tempting to use small tricks like remembering which letter comes first. We recommend using **logic** instead:

- **Thematic Flow**: Understand *why* a specific word is used based on the theme of the Surah.
- **Visual Separation**: Place similar verses in different places on your mindmap to create a clear distinction.

## Why This Matters

By addressing similar verses directly, you remove the second-guessing that often happens during recitation and make a clear decision on how to tell them apart.
