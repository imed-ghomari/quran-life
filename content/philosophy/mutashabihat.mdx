# Managing Mutashabihat

**Mutashabihat** (verses that are similar or identical) are one of the most common sources of confusion in Quran memorization. Quran Life treats them as a first-class citizen.

## Proactive Awareness

The app identifies verses that have similar counterparts elsewhere in the Quran. When you are reviewing a verse that has a "match," the app can provide **Mutashabihat-aware context**—automatically expanding the preview or providing hints until you reach a point where the similarity ends.

## Verifying Similarity

Mutashabihat (similar verses) often cause confusion. The **Todo** tab helps you verify if a mistake was due to a similarity.
- **Decision Engine**: Once you decide how to handle the similarity (Ignore, Solved by Mindmap, Solved by Note), the warning will clear from your list.
- **Distinction Notes**: Add personal hints or mnemonic devices directly in the Similarity Check section to help you differentiate similar verses.

*Many similarity issues are best [Solved by Mindmap](/docs/philosophy/strategy#3-mindmaps-and-verse-chunks) during the encoding phase.*

## Similarity Coverage

You can approach similar verse coverage in two ways:
- **Wait for Surface**: As you review and work through your daily portions, similar verses will naturally surface in the **relevant section of the Todo tab**. This allows you to address them as they become relevant to your current progress.
- **Thorough Review**: If you prefer to be more proactive, you can check them out one by one in the **relevant section of the Settings tab**. This gives you a complete overview of all similarities in your active part.

### Differentiation Strategy
When differentiating between similar verses, it is **highly recommended to use logic** rather than relying solely on cue-based tips (like letters, placements, or specific words).
- **Logical Flow**: Understand why a certain word is used in one Surah based on the context of the theme, rather than just memorizing a cue.
- **Contextual Anchoring**: Relate the specific phrasing to the overall message of the Surah. Logic-based differentiation is far more durable in long-term memory than simple mnemonics.

## Philosophy Tie-back
By addressing Mutashabihat explicitly, we reduce the **cognitive friction** of "second-guessing." Instead of falling into the same trap repeatedly, the app forces a conscious decision on how to handle the confusion.
