# Getting Started

Quran Life is designed to be intuitive, but follow these steps to get the most out of the system.

## 1. Set Your Active Part
Go to the **Settings** tab and select the Part (Juz) or "All Quran" that you are currently focusing on. This filters your Daily Portion and Todo list.

## 2. Mark Learned Surahs
In the **Settings** tab, toggle the Surahs you have already memorized. This adds them to your review pool.

## 3. Create Your First Mindmap
Navigate to the **Todo** tab.
1. Click on a Surah that needs a mindmap.
2. Add anchors (verse ranges).
3. Use the editor to draw your map.
4. Mark it as **Complete**.

## 4. Your Daily Routine
Every day, open the **Today** tab:
1. **Clear Your Reviews**: These are high-priority items due for recall.
2. **Read/Listen to Your Portion**: Maintain your relationship with the text.

## 5. Maintenance
Check the **Todo** tab periodically for:
- **Suspended Anchors**: Verses that need structural repair.
- **Similarity Checks**: Potential confusions that need your decision.
- **Incomplete Mindmaps**: Expanding your memorized territory.
