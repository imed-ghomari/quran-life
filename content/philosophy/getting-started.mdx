# Welcome to Quran Life

Quran Life is more than just a tracking app—it's a complete system for memorizing the Quran using visual memory and smart scheduling. This guide will take you from your first day to advanced mastery.

## Phase 1: Setting Up Your Territory

Before you start, you need to tell the app what you're focusing on:
1. **Set Your Active Part**: Go to **Settings** and choose the Part (Juz) you are working on. This focuses the app's Todo list and Daily Portion on your current goal.
2. **Mark What You Already Know**: Toggle any Surahs you've already memorized in the **Settings**. These will immediately enter your **Smart Review** pool.

## Phase 2: The Daily Habit (Passive Exposure)

Every day, open the **Today** tab. Your first goal is **Passive Exposure**:
- **Listen/Read Your Portion**: This is a specific set of verses the app gives you daily. It helps you "soak in" the text subconsciously before you start active memorization.
- **The Blue Dot**: A small blue dot on the "Today" icon will remind you if you haven't finished your daily listening yet.

## Phase 3: Active Memorization (Visual Mindmaps)

This is where the real work happens. Instead of just repeating verses, you will create a "map" for them.
1. **Go to the Todo Tab**: You'll see a list of Surahs that need mindmaps.
2. **Create a Mindmap**: Use the built-in editor to split the Surah into visual "chunks." 
3. **Why it Works**: Your brain remembers images and locations better than text. By placing verses on a map, you give them a "home" in your memory.
4. **Mark as Complete**: Once your map is ready, the verses move into your **Review Queue**.

*Deep Dive: [How to create effective Mindmaps](/docs/philosophy/strategy)*

## Phase 4: Long-Term Mastery (Smart Reviews)

Once you've "learned" a Surah, the app will ask you to review it periodically.
- **When to Review**: The app uses a [Smart Review System](/docs/philosophy/spaced-repetition) to show you verses just as you're about to forget them.
- **The Counters**: 
    - **Today Counter**: High-priority reviews due right now.
    - **Todo Counter**: Structural tasks (like fixing memory gaps or checking similar verses).
- **Not Sure?**: If you make a mistake but aren't sure if it was a genuine memory gap, use the **Not Sure** button. It's better than marking it as "Forgot."

## Advanced: Maintenance & Troubleshooting

As you become an advanced user, keep an eye on these sections in the **Todo** tab:
- **Memory Gaps (Suspended Anchors)**: If you fail a review 3 times, the app [Suspends it](/docs/philosophy/re-learning). This forces you to stop and fix your mindmap before moving on.
- **Similar Verses (Mutashabihat)**: The app will alert you to [Similar Verses](/docs/philosophy/mutashabihat). Advanced users use logic and thematic flow to distinguish them, rather than simple tricks.
