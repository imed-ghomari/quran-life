# Welcome to Quran Life

Quran Life helps you memorize the Quran using visual maps and steady daily reading, with reviews in Anki.

This app now has **3 tabs** at the top:

- **Daily Portion** — your daily reading or listening
- **Anki Deck** — create mindmaps, split verses into groups, and export to Anki
- **Documentation** — guides you are reading now

## 1. Daily Portion (Passive)

Open **Daily Portion** every day.

- **What you get:** a short set of verses for your current Part. Listen or read it to get familiar with the sound and flow.
- **How it works:** The app gives you about the same minutes each day and shows you the next verses in your Part. You can skip Surahs you already know well, and you can choose listening or reading, and how your reading is shown.
- **When to move on:** When the verses feel familiar after a few cycles, you are ready to create a mindmap for that Surah. If you are in a hurry you can still memorize by repeating, but create a mindmap afterwards to keep it.

## 2. Create Your Mindmap

Open **Anki Deck**, choose a Surah.

- **Use a pre-made map** if available, or **Create a new one** with the drawing editor.
- **Split the Surah** into groups that match the parts of your map. Short Surahs with 10 verses or fewer can stay as one group, or you can split them.
- When your map and splits look good, you are ready to export.

## 3. Export and Review in Anki

In **Anki Deck**, choose a Surah or export the full deck, then import the file into Anki.

- Anki will show you each group step by step. Try to recall before revealing the next part.
- Grade yourself: **Remembered**, **Not Remembered**, or **Postpone**.
- Anki schedules the next review for you. The more you remember, the less often you see it.

## Staying on Track

- **If a group keeps failing:** open its mindmap, make it clearer, or split the group smaller, save, and export again.
- **Similar verses:** some verses look alike. Your cards show which groups are related so you can learn the difference. See [Similar Verses](/docs/philosophy/mutashabihat).

Track your daily reading in the app and your review history in Anki.
