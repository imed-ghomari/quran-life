# Welcome to Quran Life

Quran Life is more than just a tracking app—it's a complete system for memorizing the Quran using visual memory and smart scheduling. This guide will take you from your first day to mastery.

## Phase 1: Setting Up Your Territory

When you first launch the app, you'll go through an **Onboarding** process to set your initial preferences. If you ever need to adjust them, you can do so in the [Settings](/docs/philosophy/settings) tab:

1.  **Set Your Active Part**: Choose the Part (Juz) you are working on. This focuses the app's Todo list and Daily Portion on your current goal.
2.  **Skipping Surahs**: You can mark Surahs you have already fully mastered (like Surah Al-Fatiha or Al-Ikhlas) as **Skipped**. This removes them from your daily portion, todo list, and reviews to prevent cluttering your workflow with things you already know perfectly.

## Phase 2: The Daily Habit (Passive Exposure)

Every day, open the **Today** tab. Your first goal is **Passive Exposure**. This ensures that you maintain a constant relationship with the entire text you are memorizing.

-   **Listen/Read Your Portion**: This is a specific set of verses the app gives you daily. Listen to it to subconsciously absorb the verses. This builds a foundation for your active memorization while you prepare your visual mindmaps.
-   **How it's Calculated**: Your portion size is determined by your **Active Part** and your **Target Completion Days** (set in Settings). We divide the total verses by your target days to get a baseline.
-   **Goal**: The intent is to complete a full reading/listening cycle of your active territory every 7-30 days.
-   **Hafs Mushaf Standard**: We use the official **Hafs Mushaf** text and alignment to ensure your memorization matches the most widely used standard.
-   **The Blue Dot**: A small blue dot on the "Today" icon will remind you if you haven't finished your daily listening yet.

It is recommended to move on to the **active learning phase** (marking it as complete for reviews) once you have completed enough **Daily Portion** cycles to feel the "sound" and general flow of the verses. Once you meet this criteria, you might need a tiny amount of rote repetition at the very end to bridge any final gaps before marking a Surah as fully learned. This should be quick and targeted. 

However, if you are in a rush, you can bypass the passive listening phase and use rote memorization to "cram" the verses into your short-term memory. Once you know the words, you **must still create or use pre-made mindmap** to lock them in.

## Phase 3: Active Memorization (Visual Mindmaps)

This is where the real work happens in the **Todo** tab. Instead of just repeating verses, you will associate them with a visual "map."

1.  **Choose Your Path**: For every Surah, you have two options:
    -   **Import a Map**: Use the pre-made, high-quality mindmap to get started immediately. This is great for speed and reference.
    -   **Edit/Create Your Own**: Use the built-in drawing tools to edit the pre-made mindmap or build your own mindmap from scratch. This provides the strongest memory encoding because you are making the structural decisions yourself.
2.  **Setup Your Verse Groups**: If creating your own, use the **Splits configuration modal to split the Surah into visual segments (we call these "chunks").
3.  **Complete the Map**: It is **crucial** to create or use existing mindmap before you start reviewing specific verses.
    -   *Why?* Without a visual map, your brain has no "place" to store the verses, leading to weak, isolated memories that fade quickly. The map provides the structure that holds the verses together.
4.  **Mark as Complete**: Once your map is ready and you feel familiar with the verses through your **Daily Portion** cycles, mark the Surah as complete to begin your **Smart Review** cycle.

> To help you situate yourself, we have added icons that appear directly on the Kanban cards themselves:
> - Brain: Custom mindmap (not an official template).
> - BadgeCheck: Official (pre-made) mindmap.
> - PenSquare: Official map edited by you.
> - Layers: Custom map, but an official reset is available.
> - scissors: Splits not created yet.

## Phase 4: Long-Term Mastery (Smart Reviews)

Once you've "learned" a Surah, the app will ask you to review it periodically.

-   **When to Review**: The app uses a [Smart Review System](/docs/philosophy/spaced-repetition) to show you verses just as you're about to forget them.
-   **Today Counter**: The red badge on the "Today" tab shows high-priority reviews due or overdue.

## Maintenance: Managing Your Tasks

As you progress, the **Todo** tab will be your hub for maintenance. Watch for the **Todo Counter**, which indicates structural tasks that need attention:

-   **Memory Gaps (Suspended Anchors)**: If you fail a review 3 times, the app [Suspends it](/docs/philosophy/re-learning). This forces you to stop and fix your mindmap before moving on.
-   **Similar Verses (Mutashabihat)**: The app will alert you to [Similar Verses](/docs/philosophy/mutashabihat). Use logic and thematic flow to distinguish them, rather than simple tricks.
