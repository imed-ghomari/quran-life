# Anki Deck & Reviews

This app does not have a review queue inside it. Your reviews happen in **Anki**.

## Where Everything Lives

- **Daily Portion tab** — daily reading or listening. This is passive and stays in the app.
- **Anki Deck tab** — this is where you prepare. Choose a Surah, create or edit its mindmap, adjust how its verses are split into groups, preview the cards, then export.
- **Anki app** — this is where you review. After you import, Anki shows you cards when they are due.

## Preparing in the Anki Deck Tab

For each Surah:

1. **Mindmap:** tap **Create Mindmap** or **Edit Mindmap** to draw. You can view it afterwards.
2. **Splits:** use **Add anchor** to add a new group, edit the start and end verse and the label, then **Save**.
3. **Preview:** check how the verses will appear as cards.
4. **Export:** export a single Surah or the full deck, then import the file into Anki.

A Surah is ready when it has a mindmap (optional but recommended) and its verses are split into groups.

## Reviewing in Anki

Open Anki each day. Cards are shown when due. Try to recall before revealing the next part of the verse.

- **Remembered** — you recalled correctly.
- **Not Remembered** — you made a mistake, you will see it sooner.
- **Postpone** — skip for a day if you are unsure.

To find cards that need fixing, use Anki's flags and tags. If a group keeps failing, go back to the **Anki Deck** tab, improve its mindmap or split it smaller, save, and export again.
