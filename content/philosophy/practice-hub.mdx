# Anki Deck & Reviews — How to Use

This app does not have a review queue inside it. Your reviews happen in **Anki** with the custom card you exported.

## Where Everything Lives

- **Daily Portion tab** — daily reading or listening. Passive, stays in the app.
- **Anki Deck tab** — prepare. Choose a Surah, create/edit its mindmap, adjust splits, preview, then export.
- **Anki app** — review. After import, Anki shows you cards when they are due.

## Preparing in the Anki Deck Tab

For each Surah:

1. **Mindmap:** `Create Mindmap` or `Edit Mindmap` (full `tldraw` `src/components/MindmapEditor.tsx`) → `Save`. `View` toggles an inline preview *under* the selector (hidden by default, above `Define Splits` for surah or above `Notes` for part/meta).
2. **Splits:** `Define Splits` → `+` / `×` per verse, `Save Splits` (`btn btn-primary`). For `≤10` verses you get 1 auto-group `1–N`; your saved splits replace it (source `private-assets/premade-mindmaps/*.chunks.txt` → `public/premade-anki-data.json` `splits`).
3. **Notes:** `Notes for this mindmap` textarea is saved on `blur` or tab switch and exported as `MindmapDocs`.
4. **Export:** `Export Full Deck to Anki` → import `.apkg` into Anki. Re-importing same deck **updates** existing notes (stable `guid` `ql-{surah}-{start}-{end}`) and keeps `Due`/`Ivl` for unchanged groups.

A Surah is ready when it has a mindmap (optional but recommended) and its verses are split.

## Reviewing a Verse-Group Card in Anki — Walkthrough

This is the core loop — **don't just flip**:

**Front:**
- Top: `ContextFront` — 2 verses before the group (or `Surah — الفاتحة (1)` for `1:1`), dashed. If the card is `tag:mutashabihat`, 3 more appear as `extra` *after* you reveal all chunks `src/lib/anki/apkgExport.ts:284`.
- Middle: `VerseChunksFront` — each verse `badge` + `span.chunk` (`blurred` → `next` dashed → `revealed`). `VerseContainer` is `62vh` scrollable, auto-scrolls to `next`.
- Bottom: `Reveal next` (`Space`) + counter `2 / 5 chunks` + hidden `doneHint`.

**Steps:**
1. **Recall `next`:** Look at context + `next` badge (e.g., `3`), try to say that whole chunk *before* revealing.
2. **Reveal:** Click `Reveal next` or `Space`. It unblurs one chunk, next becomes `next`, counter updates, scrolls.
3. **Repeat** until `doneHint` (`✓ All revealed — press Show Answer`) appears and `Reveal` hides. If `mutashabihat`, the 3 extra context verses now appear.
4. **Grade:** Press `Space` again → Anki `Show Answer` → `Again` / `Good` / `Bury` (`1`/`2`/`B`). `Good` increases interval, `Again` shows sooner, `Bury` postpones 1 day without reset.

**Back:** `RelatedGroups` (`2:23 ~ 11:45`), `MindmapDocs` (your notes), `AnchorLabel • Range — Surah`. No `VerseFull`/`MindmapImage` on verse cards (removed for speed).

**Shortcuts:** `Space` reveal → `Show Answer`, `1` Again, `2` Good, `B` Bury. Mindmap cards are separate: Front `Title`, Back `MindmapImage` + `Docs`.

## After Review — Manual Fixes

- **Leech (3× `Again`):** `Browse → tag:leech` → fix its mindmap (see [Fixing Gaps](/docs/philosophy/re-learning)), then `Cards → Forget` to make it `New` again.
- **Similar confusion:** `Flag Red` during review → `Browse → flag:1` → spread them on the map.
- **Split changed:** `Browse → deck:QuranLife tag:surah::67` → delete the old `1-30` whole card, keep the new `1-5` etc.
