# The Re-learning Phase

When a memory fails, it's not enough to just "review it again tomorrow." Quran Life implements a "Re-learning Phase" for items that have been suspended due to repeated failures.

## The 3-Strike Rule (Suspension)

If you fail to recall an anchor/verse chunk **3 times in a row**, the item is **Suspended**. 
- It is removed from your regular "Today" review queue.
- It moves to the "Fix Mindmaps" section of the **Todo** tab.

## Fixing Memory Gaps

When you reach 3 failed reviews for a specific segment, it is automatically suspended to prevent repeating mistakes.
- **Alerts**: Suspended segments appear in your **Todo** tab to alert you of a 'memory gap'.
- **Action**: Review your mental image, update the mindmap if needed, and 'Clear Issues' to resume your reviews.

## The Probation Sequence
Once you click **Confirm Fix**, the item doesn't go back to a long interval. It enters a dedicated probation:
- **Step 1 (Day 1)**: Must pass to move forward.
- **Step 2 (Day 3)**: A secondary check.
- **Graduation**: Only after passing both steps does it return to the standard SM-2 cycle (with a "Smart Recovery" interval to avoid starting from zero).

## Philosophy Tie-back
This phase ensures that you are not just "guessing" but have actually **fixed the mental structure**. It prevents the frustration of seeing the same failed item over and over without a real solution.
