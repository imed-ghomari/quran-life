# Understanding Your Stats

The **Statistics** tab provides a detailed view of your progress, broken down by mindmaps, verse chunks, and listening cycles.

## Maturity Levels

Understand the strength of your memory through maturity levels. We use the interval (days until next review) to categorize your knowledge:
- **New**: Just started (< 14 days).
- **Medium**: Starting to stick (14-30 days).
- **Strong**: Solid long-term memory (30-90 days).
- **Mastered**: Deeply encoded (90+ days).

The legend under each chart shows the specific day ranges and the count of items in each bucket.

## Filtering & Scope

Stats are dynamically updated based on your current focus:
- **Active Part**: Surah and Verse stats follow your Active Part selection from Settings.
- **Global Coverage**: Part Mindmaps always show global progress across all 4 parts.
- **Skipped Content**: Elements from surahs marked as 'Skipped' are excluded to show your true coverage.

## Chart Modes

- **Verse Chunks**: You can toggle between viewing progress by individual **Chunks** (groups of ~5 verses) or by whole **Surahs**. 
- **Future Due**: This chart shows your upcoming workload. You can see how many reviews are coming up in the next month, 3 months, or year.
