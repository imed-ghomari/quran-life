# Introduction

Welcome to the **Quran Life** documentation. This application is more than just a Quran reader; it is a unified system designed for the lifelong journey of Quran memorization and maintenance.

## The Philosophy: Beyond Rote Memorization

The core philosophy of Quran Life is to **maximize sequential Quranic verse recall with minimal cognitive friction.**

Memorizing the Quran is a monumental task that often faces two major hurdles:
1. **The Forgetting Curve**: Without structured review, what is learned is quickly lost.
2. **Cognitive Overload**: Managing what to review, when to review, and how to fix errors can become overwhelming.

Quran Life addresses these by integrating three powerful pillars:
- **Mindmap-Driven Encoding**: Visual anchors for spatial memory.
- **Spaced Repetition (SM-2)**: Scientifically optimized review schedules.
- **Mutashabihat Management**: Proactive awareness of similar verses.

### The "Life" in Quran Life
The app is designed to be a companion for life. It handles the "administrative" side of memorization—scheduling, tracking, and error analysis—allowing you to focus entirely on the sacred words of the Quran.

## Core Features

- **Today Tab**: Your daily mission. A blend of high-priority reviews and your sequential daily portion.
- **Todo Tab**: Your workshop for growth. Create mindmaps, fix suspended verses, and resolve similarities.
- **Statistics**: A mirror of your progress, showing the "maturity" of your memorization.
- **Settings**: Tailor the experience to your specific needs and schedule.
