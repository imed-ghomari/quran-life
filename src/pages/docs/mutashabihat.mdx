# Managing Mutashabihat

**Mutashabihat** (verses that are similar or identical) are one of the most common sources of confusion in Quran memorization. Quran Life treats them as a first-class citizen.

## Proactive Awareness

The app identifies verses that have similar counterparts elsewhere in the Quran. When you are reviewing a verse that has a "match," the app can provide **Mutashabihat-aware context**—automatically expanding the preview or providing hints until you reach a point where the similarity ends.

## Verifying Similarity

Mutashabihat (similar verses) often cause confusion. The **Todo** tab helps you verify if a mistake was due to a similarity.
- **Decision Engine**: Once you decide how to handle the similarity (Ignore, Solved by Mindmap, Solved by Note), the warning will clear from your list.
- **Distinction Notes**: Add personal hints or mnemonic devices directly in the Similarity Check section to help you differentiate similar verses.

## Similarity Coverage

In the **Settings** tab, you can track your overall coverage of similarity groups within your active part.
- **Resolved**: Mark a group as resolved once you've memorized the distinctions.
- **Coverage Bar**: The progress bar reflects how many identified similarity groups you have addressed in the active part.
- **Custom Pairs**: Use the 'Add Custom' button in Settings to create your own similarity comparisons between any two verses.

## Philosophy Tie-back
By addressing Mutashabihat explicitly, we reduce the **cognitive friction** of "second-guessing." Instead of falling into the same trap repeatedly, the app forces a conscious decision on how to handle the confusion.
