# Spaced Repetition (SM-2)

Quran Life uses the **SM-2 Algorithm**, the same science that powers apps like Anki, to manage your review schedule.

## The Science of Forgetting

Memories fade over time. However, if you review information just as you are about to forget it, the memory becomes much stronger, and the interval until the next review can be longer.

## The Review System

Our SM-2 algorithm ensures long-term retention by scheduling reviews at optimal intervals.
- **Reveal Chunks**: Test your memory piece by piece by clicking through verse chunks.
- **Grading**: Marking a card as 'Remembered' increases its next appearance interval.
- **Mastery**: Items eventually reach a "Mastered" state where you only need to review them once every few months.

## Daily Portion (Passive Learning)

A specialized tool to help with memorization while you prepare your visual mindmaps.
- **Passive Learning**: Listen to your daily portion to subconsciously absorb the verses. This builds a foundation for your active memorization.
- **Cycle Settings**: The portion updates daily based on your completion days to ensure you cover the entire active part.
- **Tracking**: Mark as complete once finished to clear the notification badge and move the pointer forward for tomorrow.

## Maturity Levels
We categorize your progress into four buckets based on review intervals:
- **New**: Just started (< 14 days).
- **Medium**: Starting to stick (14-30 days).
- **Strong**: Solid long-term memory (30-90 days).
- **Mastered**: Deeply encoded (90+ days).

## Philosophy Tie-back
Spaced repetition ensures **minimal wasted effort**. You don't spend time reviewing what you already know well, and you don't lose progress on what you've just learned. The app handles the "when", so you can focus on the "what".
