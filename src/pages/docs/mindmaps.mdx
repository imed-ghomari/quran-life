# Mindmap-Driven Memorization

Mindmaps are the cornerstone of the Quran Life memorization strategy. They bridge the gap between abstract text and spatial memory.

## Why Mindmaps?

The brain is naturally wired to remember **places and spatial relationships** much better than abstract lists of text. By creating a mindmap for a Surah or a Part, you are:
- **Creating Visual Anchors**: Each "chunk" of verses is tied to a specific location on a canvas.
- **Structural Encoding**: You see the hierarchy and flow of themes within a Surah.
- **Personalized Meaning**: The act of drawing or arranging the mindmap yourself creates a stronger mental bond with the verses.

## How it Works in the App

### 1. Anchoring
In the **Todo** tab, you can add "anchors" to a Surah. These are specific verse ranges that you choose to group together based on meaning or theme.

### 2. Creation
Using the integrated `tldraw` editor, you design your mindmap. You might use colors, arrows, and keywords to link the verse chunks together.

### 3. Review Integration
Once a mindmap is marked **Complete**, it begins to appear in your **Today** reviews. Instead of just seeing the text, you are prompted to recall the verses based on their position and relationship in your mindmap.

## Philosophy Tie-back
By using mindmaps, we reduce **cognitive friction** during recall. Instead of struggling to find the next verse in a vacuum, your brain "sees" where it sits on the map you created.
