Launching Quran Life for the first time

March 31, 2026 · One min read
Quran Life
Quran memorization companion

The first launch is not the moment a product becomes perfect. It is the moment the real conversation begins.

Quran Life launched at a stage where not all planned part mindmaps were ready yet. In particular, the six other parts still needed their mindmaps. That was known. The launch was still intentional, because waiting for total coverage would have delayed the more important test: does the method actually convince real users once they try it?

That was the main early challenge. Quran Life is not entering a crowded category with many similar tools proving the same method. We are basically alone in this space, which means the first job is not only shipping features. It is persuading people that this memorization technique is effective in the first place.

For that reason, the first-launch message has to be honest. It should explain what is already strong, what is still incomplete, and why the product was launched anyway. Honest launches build more trust than inflated ones.

The plan for Quran Life: goals, user milestones, and what comes next

March 31, 2026 · One min read
Quran Life
Quran memorization companion

Quran Life is not being built as a generic productivity app with Islamic branding. It is trying to become a serious memorization system — and it took its current shape deliberately: an Obsidian plugin for mapping and daily reading, with Anki doing what Anki is best at, scheduling reviews.

For now, the most important milestone is not a flashy new feature. It is trust in the method: does mindmap-first memorization with spaced review actually convince real users once they try it?

If it does, the next priorities are very clear: keep the plugin stable and low-bug, finish the pre-made mindmap collection, and keep improving the documentation until users can really understand the ins and outs of the method and the workflow.

So the roadmap is less about a long feature wishlist and more about one question: can Quran Life stay focused on the core memorization method while the tooling around it keeps getting more reliable?

Quran Life vs other Quran memorization apps

March 31, 2026 · One min read
Quran Life
Quran memorization companion

Many Quran memorization apps help you repeat. Quran Life is trying to help you structure, diagnose, and retain.

That difference matters. Repetition is necessary, but by itself it does not explain why one surah feels stable while another keeps collapsing. Quran Life is built around visual mapping, review timing, mutashabihat handling, and error-aware maintenance.

So the comparison is not really "more features" versus "fewer features." It is a different theory of memorization.

Most apps optimize for tracking activity. Quran Life tries to optimize for building a memory system.

Why I built Quran Life

March 31, 2026 · One min read
Quran Life
Quran memorization companion

I built Quran Life because I felt a gap between how serious hifdh actually is and how weak most digital tools for it felt.

Many tools help you repeat. Very few help you structure. Even fewer help you diagnose where memorization is breaking down and why.

Quran Life came from the belief that memorization should be treated as a system, not only as a motivation problem.

Structure, timing, and distinction all matter. I wanted a tool that respected that reality.
