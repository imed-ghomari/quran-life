# 67

- Verses 1-5: The ones who own everything created life on this earth as a way to test us.
- Verses 6-14: This test will result in either failure or success in the afterlife.
- Verses 15-24: Life on this earth is therefore not the end goal we should pursue.
- Verses 25-end: Those who do so will fail the test.

# 68

- Verses 1-7: The prophet is not crazy; his character has been proven by his exemplary behavior before Islam and by both the old scriptures and the future Sunnah books.
- Verses 8-16: On the opposite spectrum, those who oppose him possess terrible character.
- Verses 17-34: They are deluded like the people of the gardens who achieved worldly ruin through their ingratitude.
- Verses 35-47: Their current delusion will bring them otherworldly ruin if they continue on their present path.
- Verses 48-end: So be patient, and don't be like Prophet Yunus, who gave up before his allotted time.

# 69

- Verses 1-3: The ultimate reality has two parts.
- Verses 4-12: The punishment of this world, exemplified by the past peoples of Ad, Thamud, Pharaoh, and Lot.
- Verses 13-18: And the punishment of the next world.
- Verses 19-24: The people who receive their book of deeds on their right hand will be spared from punishment.
- Verses 25-37: And it will be reserved for those who receive their books of deeds on their left.
- Verses 38-end: These two parts of this reality (both the seen and the unseen) serve as proof that what is revealed to the prophet is the truth.

# 70

- Verses 1–7: They are questioning the punishment, not out of curiosity but only to mock this event.
- Verses 8–21: This event, however, is horrible—so horrible that it removes the very skin of those subjected to it.
- Verses 22–35: The people who are protected from this event are the people of sacrifice—those who sacrifice themselves (by avoiding shameful acts and acting responsibly) and what they possess of money and time.
- Verses 32–end: Be patient with their mockery, for Allah is above all that they say.

# 71

- Verses 1-14: Nuh made a supplication against his people because they didn't want to repent until the end.
- Verses 15-20: They didn't believe in their own creation.
- Verses 21-end: The dua consisted of removing all non-believers from the face of the earth.

# 72

- Verses 1-4: The jinn were trying to listen to the Prophet's recitation of the Quran.
- Verses 4-18: This is because they attempted to intercept this revelation from the sky, which they had done in the past, but not anymore.
- Verses 19-end: The only knowledge he can share with them is the revealed knowledge from his Lord.

# 73

- Verses 1-11: The prophet and his companions are instructed to prepare for the next stage of their journey through night prayer.
- Verses 12-19: This will help them to maintain their patience against the disbelievers, who have a poor destination.
- Verse 20: However, not all companions could uphold the command; therefore, Allah granted them some flexibility.

# 74

- Verses 1-7: The prophet is instructed to begin the propagation of the message.
- Verses 8-31: He is to warn the people of Quraysh about the punishment, emphasising that they cannot overpower the angels who will cast them into the fire.
- Verses 32-end: They are advised to cease their wrongdoings and to prepare for that day.

# 75

- Verses 1-15: The change between the two opposing emotions of following our instinct and then feeling guilty about it is proof of having accountability that will take place on the Day of Judgment.
- Verses 16-19: The prophet is different from them; he rushes, trying to seek Allah.
- Verses 20-25: While they rush towards their bad deeds.
- Verses 26-end: Until death overtakes them.

# 76

- Verses 1-3: Allah created the human being from nothing to become something worthy of a great destiny.
- Verses 4-22: Either the fire or paradise. In paradise, there are multiple levels: in the first level, you are responsible for getting the drink yourself; in the second level, you are given the drink; and in the third (highest) level, Allah gives you the drink.
- Verses 23-end: This great destiny requires patience and worship to attain.

# 77

- Verses 1–6: Through a single element—the wind—Allah can bring both life and death.
- Verses 7–15: Therefore, it is easy for Him to resurrect us.
- Verses 16–19: Look at how the people of the past were destroyed because of their denial.
- Verses 20–28: Look at how life is created and sustained.
- Verses 14–end: So do not deny the two fates.

# 78

- Verses 1-5: They question the great news that will shake them because it entails that actions have consequences.
- Verses 6-16: Because it doesn't make sense not to have any accountability in such a perfectly balanced world.
- Verses 17-end: Therefore, a change is needed, and we must work to earn the right to enter the blessed destination and avoid the wretched one.

# 79

- Verses 1–14: You will die by the hands of the angels, either harshly or gently, depending on your beliefs.
- Verses 15–26: Just like Pharaoh, who was seized harshly at his death because of his disbelief.
- Verses 27–33: So do not be like him; believe in the Day of Resurrection. You can be created again, since your own creation is easier than creating greater things (intellectual argument).
- Verses 34–end: The other barrier to embracing this belief is your attachment to this world (emotional argument).

# 80

- Verses 1-16: The Prophet was reprimanded for prioritizing the leaders of Quraysh (disbelievers) over a believer.
- Verses 17-32: They should not have been prioritized because they did not believe in the Hereafter. As a result, an argument is presented to them: let them look at their own origins and how the human life cycle resembles the life cycle of their own food
- Verses 33-end: Even though they do not care about the Day of Judgment now, on that day it will be the only matter they care about, and nothing else—even their families—will matter to them.

# 81

- Verses 1-9: When the sun loses its light (day of resurrection)
- Verses 10-18: You who use the sky for your superstitions, you will understand the real truth
- Verses 19-end: And that is the truth delivered by the powerful angel and the most perfect human being (the prophet)

# 82

- Verses 1–5: The Day of Judgment will occur when the world order is overturned.
- Verses 6–8: So why do you persist in sin and remain without fear?
- Verses 9–end: You do so because you do not believe in the recompense of that Day.

# 83

- Verses 1-6: The criminals who sin repeatedly do so because they lack any morality that would come from the belief in the day of judgment.
- Verses 7-18: As a result, they will find themselves with their book of deeds in the hellfire's lowest prison.
- Verses 19-end: In contrast, the good people are in an elevated state, where the angels admire their book of deeds.

# 84

- Verses 1-15: The shattering of the sky is a sign of the last phase.
- Verses 16-20: It will come, just like all the phases of our worldly existence inevitably come.

# 85

- Verses 1-11: There are forts of angels in the sky ready to punish those who harmed the people of the trench in the hereafter. As for the patient believers, they are rewarded.
- Verses 12-end: The punishment can also be enacted in this life. An example is the destruction of past disbelieving armies, which serves as a direct warning to Quraysh.

# 86

- Verses 1–10: Just as a star suddenly appears in the night sky and pierces through its darkness, the Day of Judgment will come suddenly and cut through the routine of your daily life, exposing all secrets.
- Verses 11–end: And just as you secretly scheme against the Prophet, Allah also has His own plan.

# 87

- Verse 1: Allah is the most high, the most perfect.
- Verses 2-5: He created the two extreme destinations to display His power.
- Verses 6-8: And He gave us the Quran.
- Verses 9-end: And He guided us through it. Those who follow will attain success, and those who do not will fail.

# 88

- Verses 1–16: On the Day of Resurrection, people will be divided into two destinies. It is called "the overwhelming event" because nothing is more important than this, including …
- Verses 17–20: This worldly life, which is meant for reflection and serving as evidence for the Day of Resurrection.
- Verses 21–end: The Prophet’s role is solely to remind people of this reality—nothing more.

# 89

- Verses 1-5: There are five times when the afterlife should be especially prioritised over this life for those who understand its importance.
- Verses 6-14: This can be achieved by avoiding wasteful expenditure.
- Verses 15-16: Or by associating wealth with one's identity.
- Verses 17-end: Or by viewing wealth as the ultimate goal, because the ultimate aim is the two destinies in the afterlife.

# 90

- Verses 1-4: Human life is challenging, as shown by the Prophet's life in Makkah.
- Verses ==5-10: As a response, a human can choose to use their current resources wastefully.==
- Verses 11-17: Or he can overcome this difficulty by investing his resources wisely.
- Verses 18-20: Doing so, he either secures his future or wastes it.

# 91

The soul, if purified, can be like the sun. But the sun can be covered by the night, which represents the soul when it is corrupted, just like the corrupted souls of the people of Thamud.

# 92

The good and bad efforts of human beings are as different as night and day, and their impact in the afterlife is also very different. Both paths are made easier for the individual because humans are creatures of habit.

# 93

The Prophet is void of life when the revelation is paused; nevertheless, Allah did not abandon him. The proof of that is his countless past blessings (shelter, guidance, self-sufficiency). Therefore, he must show gratitude by caring for those in states he once occupied (orphans and seekers).

# 94

Even though the prophet's mission was very difficult, Allah made it easier and successful for him by relieving his burdens (reducing his overwhelming sense of responsibility) and helping him understand the revelation. Both were achieved through his private worship.

# 95

The past prophets are proof of human potential to be the best creature to exist; however, people still choose to lower themselves. Therefore, the respective destinies are manifestations of Allah's justice and wisdom.

# 96

- Verses 1-5: Allah is the ultimate reality; He created us, and nothing can exist without Him.
- Verses 6-8: Most people deny this reality.
- Verses 9-end: For example, we have Abu Jahl, who tried to stop the Prophet from praying (the implementation of the realization of the ultimate reality).

# 97

The Night of Power is considered greater than a thousand ordinary nights because the Quran was revealed during it.

# 98

The people of the Book did not divide until clear proof was provided to them, represented by the prophet and his holy book. Some believed in the truth, while others disbelieved; each deserves their own fate.

# 99

The ultimate earthquake will reveal the earth's contents: the records of what we have done in secret, every small good or bad detail of our lives.

# 100

The war horses willingly put themselves in danger, showing their obedience. This contrasts with our disobedience towards our Lord out of ingratitude. How long will we remain in this state? Until our death?

# 101

The Day of Judgment will come knocking loudly, changing the reality of things to reveal the true importance of matters. One of the most important things is our deeds, which will be measured by the scale that will determine our destiny.

# 102

We always desire more from this life until we remember our death, which marks the start of the true reality that involves hellfire and the judgment of our deeds.

# 103

There is no time; we are at a loss unless we believe, do good, uphold the truth, and be patient.

# 104

Destruction of evil people who speak ill of others and refuse to relinquish their wealth, for they will be trapped in the crushing hellfire just as they have crushed others in this world.

# 105

The people of the elephant were going to destroy the Kaba, but they were instead destroyed by rocks thrown from birds.

# 106

Quraych was given food even though food is hard to cultivate on their land, and protection from the elephant without their intervention. Therefore, they should be grateful for these blessings by worshipping the provider and protector.

# 107

The denial of payback caused the people of Quraysh to commit corruption against their own community, such as oppressing orphans and the poor and refusing even small acts of kindness, as well as against Allah by neglecting their prayers and showing off.

# 108

Allah gave the Prophet much more than people say about him (childish), such as his legacy. Therefore, he/we need to worship him out of gratitude.

# 109

We can't compromise when it comes to worship. We won't worship their gods now or in the future.

# 110

When you experience success, attribute it to Allah and seek repentance, because the deeds that bring about your success will always be imperfect

# 111

Abu Lahab and his wife are in the fire, and their children do not benefit them.

# 112

We declare Allah's oneness, depend on Him while He is independent, and acknowledge that He has no family relation or anyone who can come close to Him.

# 113

We seek Allah's protection from all evils, the one who brings light after darkness, from the evil of the night, where most evil happens, particularly magic that originated from envious people.

# 114

We seek Allah's protection from the whispers of the devils, whether from the jinn or humans, by invoking three of Allah's names—Lord, King, and God—which describe His complete authority.
