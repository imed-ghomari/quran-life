# Anki Deck & Reviews

This app does not have a review queue inside it. Your reviews happen in **Anki**.

## Where Everything Lives

- **Daily Portion tab** — daily reading or listening. Passive, stays in the app.
- **Anki Deck tab** — prepare. Choose a Surah, create or edit its mindmap, adjust splits, preview, then export.
- **Anki app** — review. After import, Anki shows you cards when they are due.

## Preparing in the Anki Deck Tab

For each Surah:

1. **Mindmap:** Choose Create Mindmap or Edit Mindmap, draw your map, then Save. View shows or hides a preview under the selector (hidden by default).
2. **Splits:** Choose Define Splits, add or remove verses per group, then Save Splits. For short Surahs of 10 verses or fewer you get one automatic group covering the whole Surah; your saved splits replace it.
3. **Notes:** Write in the Notes for this mindmap box. It saves automatically and is added to your cards.
4. **Export:** Choose Export Full Deck to Anki, then import the downloaded file into Anki. Re-importing the same deck updates your existing cards and keeps review progress for unchanged groups.

A Surah is ready when its verses are split. A mindmap is optional but strongly recommended — it's your memory anchor.

## After Review — Where to Fix Things

- **Difficult card (3 times Again):** See [Fixing Mistakes](./re-learning) — find the suspended card, fix the map, put it back into rotation.
- **Similar-verse confusion:** See [Similar Verses](./mutashabihat) — flag the card, spread the two verses apart on the map.
- **Split changed:** See [How Reviews Work](./spaced-repetition) — delete the outdated group card after re-importing.

## Deck Statistics

Anki Deck → Deck Statistics shows how many Surahs and Parts have mindmaps, how many verse groups you have, and how many notes you wrote. See [Tracking Progress](./statistics) for the difference between in-app stats and Anki stats.
