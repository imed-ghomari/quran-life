# Quran Life

Welcome to Quran Life. This is a companion for memorizing the Quran with less effort and steady progress.

The plugin has **2 views**:

- **Daily Portion** — your daily reading or listening to stay familiar with the verses.
- **Anki Deck** — create mindmaps, split Surahs into groups, and export review cards to Anki. The pre-made maps (coming soon — **not ready to download yet**) use shared symbols, explained in the [Mindmap Legend](mindmaps/legend).

Reviews are done in Anki, where cards are shown step by step and scheduled for you. Start with [Getting Started](philosophy/getting-started).

---

**New here?** Open **Getting Started** for a walkthrough, then try your first Daily Portion and your first Anki export.
