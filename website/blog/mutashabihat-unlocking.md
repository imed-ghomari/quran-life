# Why mutashabihat should unlock when the verse is relevant, not all at once

If similar verses are shown too early, they become noise. If they are shown too late, confusion has already hardened.

Quran Life therefore does not try to activate every mutashabihat pair globally from day one. In practice, a mutashabihat item becomes actionable when it has actually surfaced inside your active review workflow and at least one relevant comparator is already learned enough to make the comparison useful.

That is why mutashabihat feels tied to the parent surah being relevant, not to a giant master list being dumped on you. The app is trying to show distinctions when they can reduce confusion, not when they would only create extra study overhead.
