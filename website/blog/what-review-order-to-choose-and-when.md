# What review order should you choose, and when?

Review order changes the feel of your session — and since reviews happen in Anki, the order is controlled in Anki's Deck Options, not in the plugin.

In general, the sensible default is to leave Anki's order alone: due cards first, new cards mixed in as configured. That gives the best balance between urgency and flow, and it is where new users should start.

Change it only for a specific friction. Want to stay inside one Surah's flow for a session? Use a filtered deck or a Browser preview limited to that Surah's tag. Want mindmap cards separate from verse work? Study the mindmap cards on their own day.

So the rule is simple: keep Anki's default order unless you feel a specific friction — then scope a session with a search or a filtered deck instead of reshuffling everything.
