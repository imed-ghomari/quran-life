# Why review should begin with the mindmap, not only the words

Many people think review means repeating the wording again and again until it feels stable. Quran Life starts from a different assumption: if the structure is weak, repetition alone will be fragile.

Mindmap review restores the route before it asks for perfect verbal recall.

This changes the feeling of review. Instead of starting from panic and blankness, you start from orientation. You remember where you are, what section comes next, and why the transition makes sense.

That makes the wording easier to carry.
