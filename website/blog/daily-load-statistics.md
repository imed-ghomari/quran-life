# What daily load statistics are trying to show you

Daily load is not just about how much is due today. It is about how heavy your system feels before it becomes unmanageable.

Quran Life uses that statistic to show whether your current review and study pattern is building a sustainable rhythm or quietly stacking too much pressure into future days.

The practical value is simple: it helps you judge how much new work you can safely add. If the load is already heavy, that is a sign to be conservative with new reviews or new memorization. If the load is stable, you can add more with more confidence.

This matters because hifdh often breaks down gradually, not dramatically. A useful statistics panel turns "I feel behind" into something visible enough to act on before the system becomes too heavy.
